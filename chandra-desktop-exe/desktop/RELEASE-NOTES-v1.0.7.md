# Chandra 1.0.7: Modern and Legacy

- **Pick your look in onboarding.** The first page offers **Modern** (today's Chandra: Starlight sky, the Nebula orb) or **Legacy** (Chandra's original 0.18 style). The page changes as you choose, and Settings > Interface > Style switches any time.
- **Legacy** brings back the original serif headings, the warm gold buttons, warm-tinted glass, the deep blue nebula behind everything and the coloured moon with its waveform, which breathes while Chandra listens or speaks. It sits on today's layout and all of today's features.
