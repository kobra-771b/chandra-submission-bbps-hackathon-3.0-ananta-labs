# Chandra 1.0.9: colours and location

- **Colours under both styles.** Aurora, Violet, Emerald, Rose, Amber and Mono, for Modern and for Legacy alike. Pick one in onboarding or Settings > Interface > Colour; style and colour can each be changed at any time and are remembered.
- **Location, found for you and yours to change.** A pill on Home shows where Chandra thinks you are (GPS if allowed, otherwise your approximate network location). Tap it to find your location again or type a city. A city you set always wins until you tap Find my location. Chandra now knows where you are for local questions, and the weather uses it.
